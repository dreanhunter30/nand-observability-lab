# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-18

Initial public version.

- Exact-trace NAND reconstruction with ambiguous and inconsistent outcomes.
- Passive and active examples with separate scoring references.
- A fixed 120-circuit intervention benchmark and independent checks.
- Exhaustive small-model observability experiments and minimal-circuit counterexamples.
- Input validation, command-line tests, recorded evidence, and Linux/Windows CI configuration.

This is a synthetic research prototype. Hardware acquisition, mixed gate types, sequential behavior, and general noise correction are outside its scope.
